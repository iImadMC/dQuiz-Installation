Everything you need to build & present

Designed with a crisp Windows 11 Fluent interface, smart alignment tools, and built-in AI.

🎨

19 Themes & Custom Colors

Switch between 19 curated slide themes, Light/Dark app modes, 7 accent colors, and an RGBA + Hex custom color picker.

✨

16 Animations & 11 Transitions

Bring slides to life with Zoom, Swivel, Elastic Pop, Bounce, Morph Zoom, Split, Reveal, and click-step triggers.

🧠

AI Slide & File Generator

Bring your own API key (NVIDIA, Hostinger AI Router, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, or Gemini) to generate 1–12 quiz or info slides from prompts, PDFs, text, or images.

🏆

Live Quiz Presenter Mode

Press F5 for full-screen presenting with countdown timers, live score HUD, and instant green/red answer feedback.

📐

Smart Canvas Ergonomics

Crisp fractional-DPI rendering, zoom slider & Ctrl+Wheel, smart alignment snapping guides, and arrow-key nudging.

🌍

English, Français & العربية

Switch UI language anytime in Settings with full Right-to-Left (RTL) support for Arabic and custom UI fonts.
